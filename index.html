📍 Aklan

Boracay

Boracay is a small island known for White Beach, a long stretch of powdery sand, calm shallow water, and famous sunsets. Evenings bring beachfront dining and live music.

Ask about Boracay
📍 Palawan

El Nido

El Nido sits beside the Bacuit Archipelago, where steep limestone cliffs surround hidden lagoons. Most visitors join island-hopping tours to swim, snorkel, and kayak.

Ask about El Nido
📍 Bohol

Chocolate Hills

Over 1,200 cone-shaped hills cover this part of Bohol. They are green in the wet season and turn brown in the dry season, which gives them their name.

Ask about Chocolate Hills

Why visit the Philippines?

With more than 7,000 islands, the country offers a lot of variety in one trip.

Beaches and islands

Food and festivals

Warm hospitality

Filipinos are known for welcoming visitors like family.
