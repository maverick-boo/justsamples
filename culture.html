Discover Filipino culture

The food, festivals, and everyday customs that make the Philippines feel like home.

🍲

Filipino cuisine

Filipino food mixes sweet, salty, and sour flavors, and meals are meant to be shared. Rice is served with almost everything.

  • Adobo
  • Sinigang
  • Pancit
  • Lechon
  • Kinilaw

Start with adobo, chicken or pork simmered in vinegar and soy sauce, and sinigang, a sour tamarind soup.

🎭

Festivals and traditions

Sinulog in Cebu City and Ati-Atihan in Kalibo, Aklan, are held each January to honor the Santo Niño with drums, dance, and costumes.

Pahiyas in Lucban, Quezon, takes place every May 15. Homes are decorated with colorful rice wafers, fruit, and vegetables to give thanks for the harvest.

🤝

Filipino hospitality

Bayanihan is the spirit of neighbors helping each other, and visitors often see it in village fiestas and shared meals.

You may also see mano po, a gesture where younger people touch an elder's hand to their forehead as a sign of respect.
